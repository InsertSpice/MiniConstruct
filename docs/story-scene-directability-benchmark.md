# Story Scene Plan Directability Benchmark

`story-scene-directability` is an opt-in, human-reviewed experiment at the Story Planner → Scene Plan → production H3 boundary. It asks whether a concise benchmark-only Scene Plan policy can improve downstream H3 direction while preserving story scope, pacing, appropriate stillness, and useful planning abstraction. The experimental policy and fixtures remain reproducible evidence; the reviewed principle of compact observable progression has since been incorporated into production Story Planner guidance. Production H3 remains unchanged.

Run it with two configured profiles (they may identify the same model):

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m miniconstruct.benchmark.story_scene_directability `
  --planner-profile <planner-profile> --h3-profile <h3-profile> `
  --profiles benchmarks/models.local.json
```

Results are written beneath `benchmarks/runs/story-scene-directability/`. The JSON report records each exact Planner request and reply, projected plans and authority fields, production H3 requests and outputs, structural validation, safe reference provenance, effective Planner and H3 profile settings, and descriptive lengths/timestamps. Image payloads are omitted from saved model requests while their asset provenance and digest are retained. Reports contain no API keys.

Each case runs two Story Planner Build arms from identical isolated fixture Projects and the same production request. CONTROL uses the exact current production Story Planner assembly. EXPERIMENTAL appends only the benchmark-only Scene Plan directability policy. The Planner seed is fixed to the same per-case `plannerSeed` in both arms, regardless of the source profile's seed mode; the source and effective settings are recorded separately. Both exact raw replies and fully parsed results remain in `results.json`, along with a descriptive per-Generation diff for Scene Plan, duration, dialogue, continuity states, relationship, Camera Intent, and references. This keeps unexpected Planner spillover visible.

The downstream comparison deliberately isolates Scene Plan prose. CONTROL applies the complete Control projected draft. EXPERIMENTAL begins with a copy of that complete Control draft and replaces only `scenePlan` for every planned item, matched by stable `planItemId`. Thus Experimental downstream authority retains every ordered Experimental Scene Plan, while dialogue, duration, states, relationship, Camera Intent, references, item identity/order, Sequence Context, and saved snapshot authority come from Control. The normalized draft is applied through the ordinary ProjectStore Apply path in its own temporary Project, then ordinary `story_h3_context`, H3 continuity, narrative mapping, and `generate_workspace_prompts` construct the H3 request. The harness compares the complete safe model-facing H3 messages after substituting all ordered Control Scene Plans with their corresponding Experimental plans; any other difference fails the run. H3 current and later effective durations must also match. The raw Experimental Planner result remains separately reviewable and is never presented as the normalized downstream authority. No experimental H3 policy is included.

The compact experimental policy asks for observable progression where Story authority supports it, while protecting intentional quiet, later-Generation boundaries, and the Scene Plan’s planning-level abstraction. It does not direct shots, timing, camera, soundscape, reference syntax, or animation. Human review covers Story fidelity, Generation boundaries, directability, temporal progression, stillness, invention discipline, downstream usefulness, abstraction, and verbosity, followed by H3 structure, temporal information, static padding, action, spatial clarity, camera usefulness, stillness, fidelity, invention discipline, and verbosity. There is no LLM judge or automatic creative-quality score.

The initial Gemma 4 live run gave encouraging human-reviewed evidence: more directable Scene Plans led ordinary production H3 to express clearer action and attention progression without an H3 policy change. The quiet interpersonal fixture retained appropriate pauses and stillness; the lantern interaction fixture gave clearer physical progression. This is limited evidence from reviewed Gemma 4 outputs, not a universal model-quality claim. A Qwen3.8 Q5 reasoning-on local Planner run did not complete because the backend reached its 20-minute first-token timeout, so it supplied no behavioral result for this policy.

Fixtures cover the beginning of a city key search, a quiet platform exchange, and a doorway lantern handoff. They are text-only and use no external benchmark images. The Story Planner and H3 reasoning settings are selected and recorded independently from their configured profiles; this suite does not run a reasoning-mode experiment.

The prior `h3-temporal-directing` benchmark remains available. Its initial live review across Gemma 4 and Qwen3.8 did not justify promoting the H3 overlay: effects were model-dependent, some outputs became more verbose or inventive, and one Qwen Ref2VA output lost required Picture provenance. Those observations motivate the upstream Scene Plan hypothesis; they do not show that temporal directing is generally harmful.
